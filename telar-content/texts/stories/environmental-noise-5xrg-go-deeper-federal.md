title: "Go deeper: Federal Standards"
---

At the same time at the federal level under President Nixon, the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 (NEPA) and the Noise Control Act of 1972 (NCA) created national standards for identification and restriction of environmental hazards or pollution. NEPA which was passed just before CEQA created the standards for creating an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) for proposed projects outlining its effects on local environments as a response to the widespread concern over pollution of the 1960s.
