Isla Vista (I.V.) is an unincorporated community adjacent to the University of California, Santa Barbara. Because it remains unincorporated, local governance stays in the hands of the County. There have been various pushes over time from I.V. locals to incorporate the community to instate self-governance of the community for its own representation, however, to date I.V. remains unincorporated.
