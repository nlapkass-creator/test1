SB Oil Spill- One local example of a highly publicized instance of pollution is the 1969 oil spill off the coast of Santa Barbara. An oil pipe off the coast of Santa Barbara burst on January 28th, 1969 sending hundreds of thousands of gallons of oil into the water and across the local beaches. The incident became so prominent in the media that newly inaugurated President Richard Nixon visited the site to reassure the public of his support of protecting the environment. This incident could be considered one particular turning point in the president's support of environmental legislation to assuage public concerns.
