Before the local noise ordinance took effect many I.V. residents were skeptical of the adoption of these guidelines. Opponents cited concerns over actual local support for this initiative as well as negative effects on the large local music scene. At the same time, local officials cited the ordinance as a middle ground for residents with noise complaints and local musicians, highlighting local tensions between permanent and temporary residents.
