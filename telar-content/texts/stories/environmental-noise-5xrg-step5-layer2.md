The general noise ordinance and festival ordinance are still in effect in I.V. and in 2026 the festival ordinance was expanded to also include another local party tradition "Deltopia" which occurs during the Spring. Pushback from local residents to the policing of the ordinance has remained consistent citing arbitrary citations or poor interactions with police as well as the restrictions on local musicians.
